# LinkedIn Projects Entry

## Project name

**Motor Insurance Pricing & Risk Analytics**

## Description

Developed an end-to-end motor insurance pricing and portfolio analytics project using the freMTPL2 French Motor Third-Party Liability dataset. Analysed policy exposure, claim frequency, claim severity and key risk factors including driver age, vehicle characteristics, Bonus-Malus, fuel type and area.

Applied a frequency-severity framework to calculate indicated pure premium and risk relativities, and prepared an interpretable Poisson GLM for claim frequency and Gamma GLM for claim severity. Built an Excel pricing workbook and management dashboard to translate actuarial outputs into clear portfolio insights.

**Tools:** Python | Excel | SQL | Pandas | Statsmodels | Statistical Modelling | Insurance Pricing | Risk Analytics
